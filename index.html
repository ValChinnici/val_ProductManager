Game 2: Find the job

Four discovery calls, one agentic AI roadmap

Beefree's MCP beta feedback was scattered. I used Jobs-to-be-Done to find what partners were really hiring it for.

  • AI PM and lead researcher, Beefree
  • 4 enterprise and growth-stage partners
  • Jobs-to-be-Done forces analysis

Which job is each partner hiring the MCP to do?

“Headless MCP is something that we would value a lot… many use cases require producing content offline.”
Implementation lead, enterprise partner

“We need to severely restrict the creativity of this agent. It’s not allowed to change the structure.”
Compliance lead, regulated-industry partner

“I’m not a coder… I’ve pretty much got an enterprise-level production-ready software that I’ve vibe coded.”
Non-technical founder, SMB partner

That's what I found too

Those three jobs became six roadmap hypotheses: headless capability, deterministic guardrails, AI-ready docs, tool grouping, server-side template analysis, and model and EU-hosting choice.

The big shift: headless went from nice-to-have to the top enterprise blocker. It now drives the next roadmap cycle.

Game 3: Wire the kit

A physics lab for middle schools, with Google

Middle schools couldn't afford lab gear. We built custom hardware that plugged straight into Google's Science Journal app.

How I ran it

Hardware ran on Kanban and waterfall for its long lead times; content, design and the Android app ran Agile, so firmware could keep learning from content tests. I led the Google negotiation and coordinated both companies' engineers to a public launch date. Content shipped in six languages.
