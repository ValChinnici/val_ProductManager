Level 2

Find the job

Beefree's MCP beta feedback was scattered. I used Jobs-to-be-Done with four partners to find what they were really hiring it for.

  • AI PM and lead researcher, Beefree
  • 4 enterprise and growth-stage partners
  • Jobs-to-be-Done forces analysis

Which job is each partner hiring the MCP to do?

“Headless MCP is something that we would value a lot… many use cases require producing content offline.”
Implementation lead, enterprise partner

“We need to severely restrict the creativity of this agent. It’s not allowed to change the structure.”
Compliance lead, regulated-industry partner

“I’m not a coder… I’ve pretty much got an enterprise-level production-ready software that I’ve vibe coded.”
Non-technical founder, SMB partner

Level clear

Those three jobs became six roadmap hypotheses: headless capability, deterministic guardrails, AI-ready docs, tool grouping, server-side template analysis, and model and EU-hosting choice.

Big shift: headless went from nice-to-have to the top enterprise blocker. It now drives the next roadmap cycle.
